Rates & Notes

Rates

Regular Rate → $50/Daycare & $60/Daycare + Evening.

Daycare + After Hours: $90 - Flat Rate.

Discounted Rates

Pay for daycare in advance to unlock discounts!

Prepay 1–3 days → $45/Daycare & $50/Daycare + Evening.

Prepay 4+ days → $40/Daycare & $45/Daycare + Evening.

To activate your discount, select your desired daycare date below and check the "Pay in advance" box.

Notes

When you pay in advance for a week, any bookings made after are counted as regular rate for that week.

Daycare booked within 24 hours of drop off will not count towards the discounted rate.

Although, discounted boarding rates will be upheld even if you book within 24 hours.

Payment will be collected separately via phone or in person.

A week is Monday through Sunday.

The price of boarding is a flat rate per night. However, based on your prior month of scheduling you will qualify for a discounted boarding rate. Even if you schedule within 24 hours.

Cancellations

You can cancel at any time.

Any cancellations made after payment will be counted towards redeemable tickets accepted up to 30-days after cancellation.

Tickets will be automatically applied when booking future services.

Boarding cancellations must be made 72 hours in advance.

Rates & Notes

Rates

Regular Rate → $50/Daycare & $60/Daycare + Evening.

Daycare + After Hours → $90 flat rate.

Discounted Rates

Pay for daycare in advance to unlock discounts.

Prepay 1–3 days → $45/Daycare & $50/Daycare + Evening.

Prepay 4+ days → $40/Daycare & $45/Daycare + Evening.

To activate the discount, select daycare dates below and check Pay in advance.

Notes

When you pay in advance for a week, any bookings made after are counted as regular rate for that week.

Daycare booked within 24 hours of drop off will not count toward the discounted rate.

Discounted boarding rates are still upheld even if booked within 24 hours.

Payment will be collected separately via phone or in person.

A week is Monday through Sunday.

Boarding is priced as a flat nightly rate. Based on your prior month of scheduling, you may qualify for discounted boarding rates, even within 24 hours.

Cancellations

You can cancel at any time.

Any cancellations made after payment will be counted toward redeemable tickets accepted up to 30 days after cancellation.

Tickets will be automatically applied when booking future services.

Boarding cancellations must be made 72 hours in advance.
